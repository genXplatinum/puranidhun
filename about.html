And this site

Purani Dhun is three rooms of records and a machine to play them with. Nine rooms of old Hindi film songs on the front page; Loha, 499 Punjabi records across four decks, for lifting; and Max, 202 records that gyms play everywhere, in any language.

It is a static site — no build step, no framework, no tracking. The corridor behind this text is one WebGL shader. Every record streams from its own official source on YouTube; nothing is hosted here.

One thing worth saying plainly: the pages cannot hear the music. The player is a cross-origin frame, so no page here has access to the audio. What moves on the beat is a tempo clock driven by playback position, and the Tap key sets its real tempo. Nothing on this site is pretending to listen.
